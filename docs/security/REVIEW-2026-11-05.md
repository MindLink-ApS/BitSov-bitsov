## Low — fixed in this PR

### L1 — Security notes still say device approval clamps per-call to 6 sats before the signature

`docs/security/device-keys.md` and `docs/SPEND_BUDGET_GRANTS.md`. Before this PR the device-key note said an owner-device `spend_grant` is narrowed to 6,000 msat before the owner is asked to sign, and that `GET /api/v1/pair/grant` and `konsensus pair-status` expose that narrowed figure as `per_call_max_msat`. The spend-grant note said the grant view reports that same effective limit.

`#493` stopped doing that. `ApprovalRequest::normalize` (`crates/konsensus-api/src/pairing/approval.rs`) stores and signs the requested per-payment value, or the whole budget when the field is omitted, and still refuses a value above the budget. Messages, files, rooms, first contact and liquidity fees then take `min(stored value, 6_000)` at reservation (`GrantBudget::messaging_per_call_max_msat`). Call offers and blocks use the signed value, or a relation envelope's per-act maximum. The grant view's `per_call_max_msat` is the signed value. The non-call cap is `message_per_payment_max_msat`. `pair-status` already prints both. rc21 already says this. These two notes did not.

An operator or an app author who trusted the security note would treat a removed clamp as still in force, and would read the signed field as the message cap.

**Fix.** Both notes now say what is signed, what messages may spend, and what a call may spend. No reservation rule changes.

## Info

**I1 — Omitting `per_call_max_msat` on a device spend grant signs the whole budget.** The field used to be required. It is now optional and defaults to `budget_msat` before the approval text is shown. The signed `bitsov-owner-approval-v1` line contains that number, and `normalized` still rejects a value above the budget. A client cannot change it after the node issues the operation. Not changed.

**I2 — A callee-side unpaid expiry still notifies as the owner.** `MeteredSpend::call_timeout` uses the owner meter when the hangup reason is `Unpaid`, which `expire_calls` returns only for a callee entry. A grant-funded outgoing call does not take that branch. Duration-cap closure draws on the originating grant, and a missing grant provenance does not fall back to the owner wallet. Not changed.

## Checked, not a finding

- `/auth/local` still mints only `read` and `receive`, and only after loopback and the Host/Origin guard. `/auth/token`, pairing confirmation, device-key enrollment, and the locked unlock path are unchanged since `0e10897`. A paired token is still re-checked on every request. `ScopedAuth<Spend>` still refuses a paired caller on an unmetered money route.
- A device cannot approve its own pairing. The approval route still requires a different pairing's enrolled owner key, that pairing's token, and its Noise session. Self-approval is covered by `self_approval_and_borrowed_key_and_missing_noise_grant_nothing`.
- First contact is still capped by `messaging_per_call_max_msat` and by `FIRST_CONTACT_MAX_MSAT`. It is not a call payment and does not use the new call ceiling.
- Messaging grants (`owner_approval` set, or `recipients_only`) still cannot fund an explicit invoice or keysend. Console grants remain bounded by the stored per-call value on those routes.
- A new call's hold and its offer are checked and reserved in one ledger write. A refusal of either keeps neither the hold nor a breaker count. The offer amount is the callee's quoted price, not the caller's cap. The cap can only refuse a larger quote.
- A hold whose per-block ceiling no longer fits the grant is closed before another draw, inside the draw, at dispatch, and on the call sweep. What it has not paid is released. There is no fee-paying fallback.
- The closing allowance must fit the signal limit (`min(signed call limit, 6_000)` msat, or the relation per-act maximum held to that message cap) before the hold is kept. The closing draw pays that allowance from the same grant. A lowered grant refuses the draw before the wallet is polled.
- Direct-channel detection requires an active channel to the peer's Lightning key, compared case-insensitively, whose known outbound capacity covers the principals. Unknown capacity or a failed listing keeps the ordinary fee ceiling. A zero fee is the payment's fee limit.
- `call_spend_grants` records which grant funded a call so a later closing signal cannot be charged to the owner after that grant is revoked. The map is not itself spend authority. Entries are dropped when the call row is gone. A full map refuses a new hold.
