## Medium — open

### M1 — A direct payment's grant reservation stays held after the outcome is known

`direct::run` and `direct::replay` (`crates/konsensus-api/src/handlers/payments/direct.rs`). A paired caller of `POST /payments/pay` or `POST /payments/keysend` reserves principal plus the routing ceiling before dispatch. `resolve_payment` runs on that first answer only. `settle_briefly` waits 3 seconds (`SETTLE_WAIT`). An answer that is still `InFlight` leaves the reservation pending. `replay` later reads the backend and `conclude` stores `settled` or `failed` on the operation row. It does not call `resolve_payment`. The reservation id is not on that row, so the replay has nothing to resolve.

The wallet is not charged a second time: the same `request_id` does not dispatch again, and the grant's `payment_ids` still refuse the same payment hash. What sticks is the grant. `used_msat` keeps the reserved total, including a fee ceiling the settled payment did not spend, and a failed payment's whole reserve. The pending row still counts as an unresolved payment (`GrantBudget::breaker_status`). The default breaker allows 5 unresolved payments. Five direct payments that are still in flight at 3 seconds, including ones that later settle, fill that allowance. Further reservations return `FailuresPending` until the grant expires (at most 24 hours) or the owner revokes it.

The owner's own key is unmetered and is unaffected. Message and call sends go through the outbox journal, which does reconcile. This hole is the direct pay and keysend ledger added in `#442`.

**Fix (not applied).** Persist the reservation id on the operation before dispatch, and on a replay whose backend report is terminal, settle the actual fee or release a confirmed failure. Releasing on a missing or non-terminal report would let the grant fund another payment while the first HTLC may still settle. That change is larger than this pass.

## Low — fixed in this PR

### L1 — Invoice payee comparison treated hex case as a different key

`create_payment_proof_via_invoice` and `validate_admission_invoice` (`crates/konsensus-api/src/handlers/messages/compose.rs`). When `peer_ln_pubkeys` holds a key, the BOLT11 payee must match it before anything is paid. The comparison was `!=` on the display strings.

`handle_lightning_info_received` stores the peer's string. A compressed key is accepted in either hex case. BOLT11 `recover_payee_pub_key` displays lowercase. LDK's keysend parses the stored hex and accepts either case, and call offers try keysend first. Call blocks do not: they pay only the callee's invoice (`create_metered_payment_proof`). An uppercase `LightningInfo` therefore lets the offer keysend settle and then refuses every block invoice, and it refuses an admission invoice for the same key. The refusal is before `pay_invoice`. A different payee is still refused.

The 2026-10-25 channel check had the same case split and was fixed in `#430`. These two invoice checks were not.

**Fix.** `invoice_payee_is` compares with `eq_ignore_ascii_case`. `known_recipient_fallback_pays_matching_payee_regardless_of_hex_case` stores the uppercase form of the recipient key, falls through from keysend, and pays that recipient once. The third-party invoice is still refused with nothing paid.

## Info

**I1 — A new call hold is checked against the largest active channel on the node.** `with_new_call_spend` (`crates/konsensus-api/src/calls.rs`) takes `max(outbound_capacity_msat)` over every active channel. The comment says one channel must cover the hold. The figure is not the channel to the payee. A hold can be reserved when only some other peer's channel is that large. The grant total, the per-block ceiling, and the routing ceiling still bind what is paid. A zero routing fee is pinned only when `direct_channel` sees the payee's own channel carry the principals. Left as it is.

**I2 — LDK channel capacity and the punishment reserve still multiply sats by 1000 with `*`.** `channel_info` (`crates/konsensus-lightning/src/ldk.rs`). Same note as I2 in the 2026-10-25 report. The new `outbound_capacity_msat` field is LDK's millisatoshi value and is not multiplied again. LND's outbound figure uses `checked_mul(1000)` and returns no capacity when the reserve or unsettled balance is missing, so `direct_channel` stays false. Not changed.

## Checked, not a finding

- A call draw refuses a price or fee above the pinned per-block ceiling, a sequence at or past `max_call_minutes`, and a sequence already paid or in flight (`draw_call_spend_for_grant`). The closing draw is `u32::MAX`, which block sequences cannot reach. Its amount must fit `closing_allowance_msat` and the current signal limit, which is the per-payment value held to 6_000 msat.
- `check_call_closing` refuses a new hold whose closing quote does not fit that signal limit, before the hold is reserved (`#500`).
- Messaging grants and ordinary messages still reserve through `messaging_per_call_max_msat`. Explicit pay and keysend use the owner's signed per-payment value. A call hold uses that value for each block, and the whole hold still has to fit the remaining budget (`reserve_call_at`).
- A direct-channel send sets the routing ceiling to 0 and passes that 0 into LDK as `max_total_routing_fee_msat: Some(0)` and into LND as `fee_limit_msat`. It is not omitted. LDK's default (1% plus 50_000 msat) applies only when the override is absent. LNbits has no fee-limited pay and returns `PaymentNotDispatched`.
- Call blocks do not keysend. The invoice amount must equal the amount this node requested. The callee's gate still requires the settled amount to meet the kind-404 price, and `has_call_block_invoice` requires the hash to be bound to that peer, call, and sequence.
- A second envelope with a burned payment hash is `PaymentProofReused`. A block whose hash was not bound is refused before the proof is consumed.
- Channel open still rejects `amount_sats` above `u64::MAX / 1000`, rejects `push_sats` above half the capacity, and converts push with `checked_mul(1000)`. Close and force-close still require `ScopedAuth<Spend>`.
- Settled LND payments that omit `value_msat` or disagree with the invoice amount still return `LightningError::Backend` on the pay path and do not release the grant.
